About Us

THE HIMALAYA HEAVEN is a premier 5-star hotel that offers an exceptional blend of royal and traditional elegance. Set amidst 12 acres of lush green landscaped area, it boasts a magnificent view of the Himalayas. Located in the peaceful Tahachal area, it’s just 8 km from the Tribhuvan International Airport and 4 km from the city center, making it conveniently accessible for travelers.

The hotel’s architecture is authentically Nepali, featuring 285 beautifully appointed heritage and modern rooms. It is home to the city’s largest casino and spa, providing guests with a variety of leisure activities. The Himalayas Kathmandu also offers a wide range of fine dining options, including authentic Nepali, Chinese, Indian, and Italian cuisines, catering to diverse palates.

For business and events, the hotel has brilliantly designed meeting spaces, making it an ideal destination for both business and leisure. The HIMALAY Kathmandu has been a cornerstone in the Nepali hospitality industry since 1966, consistently setting the standard for service and elegance. With a legacy spanning over five decades, the hotel’s spaces, from luxurious accommodations to sophisticated dining venues, showcase a seamless fusion of tradition and modernity.

Guests can indulge in various experiences such as fitness and wellness at the Tranquility spa, a revitalizing workout at the fitness center, relaxation at the swimming pool, or a game of lawn tennis. The hotel’s inviting atmosphere is often described as warm and inviting, complete with cozy vibes and live music, enhancing the overall guest experience.

The HIMALAYA HEAVEN is not just a hotel but a piece of Nepal’s rich history and a testament to its evolving tourism industry. It’s naturally the preferred hotel for heads of states and dignitaries visiting Kathmandu, reflecting the mountainous beauty of the Kathmandu Valley within its expansive grounds.
