Top Notch Bed Rooms

The rooms at The HIMALAYA HEAVEN are designed to provide guests with a luxurious bedding and comfortable stay. Each room features elegant decor, and is equipped with modern amenities such as a TV, fast internet and minibar. Selected rooms offer the added luxury of a flat-screen TV and breathtaking views of the mountains or pool. The private bathrooms are well-appointed with a bathtub, ensuring a relaxing experience.

We also emphasize any unique offerings specific to certain room categories, such as private balconies or Jacuzzi tubs.
